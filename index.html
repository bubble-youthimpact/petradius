Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about PetRadius and adopting a pet.

What is PetRadius?

PetRadius is a platform that connects potential adopters with local animal shelters and rescue organizations. We make finding adoptable pets easier through detailed pet profiles, stories, and search tools.

Is PetRadius free to use?

Yes! PetRadius is completely free for people looking to discover adoptable pets and learn more about animal adoption.

Does PetRadius operate as an animal shelter?

No. PetRadius is an online platform that helps connect people with shelters and rescue organizations. All adoptions are handled directly through the respective organizations.

Where does PetRadius get its pet listings?

Our listings come from animal shelters and rescue organizations. We work to share accurate and updated information about available pets.

How do I adopt a pet I find on PetRadius?

Once you find a pet you’re interested in, visit the associated shelter’s profile or website to learn about its adoption process and submit an application.

Can I meet a pet before adopting it?

Yes! Most shelters allow potential adopters to schedule a meeting with an animal before making an adoption decision. Contact the shelter directly for details.

What should I consider before adopting a pet?

Consider your lifestyle, living situation, available time, financial responsibilities, and the long-term commitment of caring for an animal.

Are all pets listed on PetRadius still available?

Availability can change quickly as pets get adopted or placed on hold. We recommend contacting the shelter directly to confirm a pet’s current availability.

Can I adopt a pet from a shelter outside my area?

Adoption policies vary by organization. Some shelters accept applications from people outside their local area, while others have geographic restrictions.

Can my animal shelter partner with PetRadius?

Absolutely! We welcome partnerships with animal shelters and rescue organizations interested in increasing their visibility and helping more animals find homes.

Does PetRadius charge shelters to be featured?

No. Our goal is to provide shelters with free promotional opportunities and help increase exposure for their adoptable animals.

How can my shelter submit pets to PetRadius?

Shelters interested in being featured can contact our team to discuss partnership opportunities and the process of submitting pet profiles.

Can shelters request specific animals to be featured?

Yes! We especially encourage shelters to share animals that have been waiting longer for adoption or need additional visibility.

How can I search for specific pets?

You can use our search filters to narrow down available pets based on factors such as location, breed, age, and size.

Why does PetRadius include individual pet stories?

We believe every animal has a unique personality and background. Sharing their stories helps potential adopters connect with pets beyond their basic information.

What should I do if I notice incorrect information on a pet profile?

Please contact us with the pet’s name and the information that needs updating so we can look into it.

Ready to Change a Life?

Whether you are looking to adopt, foster, or volunteer, there is a place for you in our community.
