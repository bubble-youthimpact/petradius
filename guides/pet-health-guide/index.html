Pet Health & Veterinary Guide

Proactive medical care protects your pet against preventable diseases and helps catch health concerns early.

GuidesProductsWebsites & Videos
All guides
View original

1Essential vaccinations

Core — essential

Rabies, DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza)

Non-core — lifestyle-based

Bordetella (kennel cough), Lyme, Leptospirosis

Core — essential

Rabies, FVRCP (Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, Panleukopenia)

Non-core — lifestyle-based

FeLV (Feline Leukemia Virus)

2Routine preventive care

Flea, tick & heartworm prevention

Give monthly oral or topical preventatives year-round. Heartworm is spread by mosquitoes and needs lifelong prevention.

Annual wellness exams

Yearly vet visits for adult pets (twice a year for seniors 7+) for fecal tests, dental checks and routine bloodwork.
