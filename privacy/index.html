Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who we are

PetRadius (“PetRadius,” “we,” “us”) is a website that helps people discover adoptable pets listed by animal shelters and rescues and learn about responsible pet ownership. You can contact us at petradius.official@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Account details: when you create an account with email and password, we collect your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Profile details (optional): location, phone number and an “About me” description, if you choose to add them.
  • Activity you save: the pets you mark as favorites and the results of quizzes you complete while logged in.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email address, subject and message when you contact us.

Information from Google Sign-In. If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, as shown on Google’s consent screen. We do not request access to your contacts, Gmail, Google Drive, calendar or any other Google data.

Information stored on your device. We use your browser’s local storage to remember basic settings, such as whether you are signed in. Firebase Authentication may also store sign-in information on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your profile, saved favorite pets and quiz history back to you.
  • To respond to messages you send through our contact form.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with shelters or other third parties for marketing.

Google user data

PetRadius’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create your PetRadius account, sign you in and display your name and picture on your profile.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide the service (our hosting and database provider, Firebase), to comply with the law, or with your permission.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your permission, for security purposes, or to comply with the law.

How information is shared

We share information only with the service providers that help us run PetRadius:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account, profile, favorites and quiz data. See Firebase’s privacy information.
  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our inbox. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and Google Fonts provides its typeface; like most web services, they may receive your IP address and basic browser information when pages load.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

When you click “Visit shelter” or other outside links, you leave PetRadius. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Storage and security

Your account data is stored with Google Firebase. Database security rules allow each signed-in user to read and change only their own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. You can edit your profile details at any time on your profile page and remove favorites whenever you like.

To delete your account and all associated data (profile, favorites and quiz history), email us at petradius.official@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will delete it within 30 days. Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to you.

If you signed in with Google, you can also remove PetRadius’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing petradius.official@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor requests as required by applicable law.

Children’s privacy

PetRadius is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us at petradius.official@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, email us at petradius.official@gmail.com.
