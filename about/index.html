Our Mission

PetRadius was born from a simple belief: every pet deserves a loving home, and every home deserves a loving pet. We partner with local shelters to showcase their wonderful animals, share their unique stories, and make the adoption process as warm and welcoming as possible.

What We Stand For

The values that guide our work every single day.

Compassion First

Every animal deserves dignity, care, and love. We put the wellbeing of pets at the center of everything we do.

Trust & Transparency

We believe in honest communication between shelters and adopters. No hidden details, no surprises.

Community Driven

Our platform is built by animal lovers, for animal lovers. Together we create a stronger network of care.

Sustainable Impact

We work toward reducing shelter overcrowding and promoting responsible, lifelong pet ownership.

Our Team

Ryan Choi

Founder & CEO

Growing up, I always loved animals, so adopting a dog was something my family had always wanted to do. When we decided to adopt our dog from Korea, I realized how difficult and confusing the process could be. Information about shelters, available pets, and adoption requirements was often scattered across different websites, making it hard to find trustworthy and up-to-date information. That experience inspired me to create PetRadius, a website designed to make pet adoption easier by connecting people with shelters and adoptable pets in one place. My goal is to help more animals find loving homes while making the adoption process simpler, faster, and more accessible for families everywhere.
